TOP GUN HD v0.8.0 — DEVELOPMENT REVIEW

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- Mission 2 reuses the approved ocean, sky, runway, cockpit and combat artwork.
- Painted boats and submarines follow native positions and distance sizes.
- Updated Mission 2 tactical map and briefing/weapon panels.
- Painted refueling tanker and native-timed extending boom.
- Refueling sky, cockpit and live guidance remain visible instead of black.
- Preserves all 132 approved v0.7.3 PNGs, including danger and landing fixes.

INSTALL
Extract this ZIP directly into the HD pack folder for your Top Gun (USA) ROM.
The folder must match the ROM filename without .nes (for example,
Top_Gun_HD.nes uses HdPacks/Top_Gun_HD/). hires.txt must be directly inside
that folder. Enable HD packs and reopen the ROM in MesenCE.

Tested with the user-supplied MesenCE 2.2.1 Linux build. No ROM or emulator
is included. Native gameplay, damage rules, timing and audio are unchanged.
This build covers the supplied Mission 2 recording through refueling controls.
Refueling completion, later encounters and unrecorded failures need more work.

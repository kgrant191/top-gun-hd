Built-in image generation tool, six project assets. Masters are preserved

TITLE
Create production game artwork, a complete title background for a faithful
high-definition remake of the NES game Top Gun. Canvas 1024 x 960 (16:15).
Premium hand-painted realistic 1990s arcade aviation art, crisp detail, no
pixelation. Centered upper half: iconic TOP GUN winged insignia, silver metal
letters, symmetrical red/navy striped wings and small central star, HD below.
F-14 Tomcat banking toward viewer in lower left/middle; twin tails, swept
wings, navy-grey metal, lit cockpit, aircraft carrier below on ocean. Dawn
light, deep navy sky, warm pale gold horizon, restrained red accents. Bottom
140 pixels quiet dark negative space for separate UI. No other text, menu,
watermark, border or monitor frame.

CARRIER
Original capture 00900.png used as a composition reference. Repaint as
finished 1024x960 HD game screen: large F-14 rear three-quarter view facing
away toward right, twin tails, swept wings, landing gear on deck. Aircraft
almost full width; tail tips one-third down, wheels about 72 percent down.
Low camera behind aircraft. Realistic hand-painted arcade aviation art,
navy-grey aluminum, twin engines with magenta-orange afterburners, two tiny
deck crew near edges, warm pale-gold sunset sky, textured flight deck and
runway lines. Entire canvas, no letterboxing, text, logos or interface.

COCKPIT
Original capture 03240.png used as layout reference. Repaint screenshot at
1024x960, preserving scaled instrument positions. Sky top 128/240 pixels;
cockpit begins y128. Central radar x80..175,y144..207 blank dark green.
Left digital readouts x8..47,y144..159 blank near-black. Left attitude
x24..63,y176..207 blank green. Right weapon x208..247,y144..167 blank black.
Two circular gauges centered (200,192),(232,192), radius14, and lower gauge
(216,232), detailed metal bezels but blank interiors. Full width cockpit
y136 down, central coaming y128. Charcoal graphite, steel blue-grey metal,
subtle wear, screws, panel seams, beveled frames, warm edge highlights.
Uniform pale blue sky. No numbers, letters, needles, radar grids, crosshair,
labels, pilot, hands, joystick or additional controls. Flat game artwork.

Integration notes: generated aspect ratios are normalized by the build.
Cockpit vertical zones are aligned to native instrument rows. Live data
comes from native tile replacements. Decorative dial ticks and title Start
prompt are vector/text UI generated by the build, not imagegen lettering.

TOP GUN HD v0.13.0 — DEVELOPMENT REVIEW

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- Single refueling tanker on arrival and departure in Mission 4.
- HD boom, connection, release and native control displays.
- Night red desert and dark rocky terrain with native bank and scroll.
- Clean terrain edges through transitions, damage and retry.
- Ground targets reuse the approved tank, radar and barricade artwork.
- Added close boat sizes, opposite bank poses and small fighter views.
- Preserves all 216 v0.12.0 PNGs and earlier Mission 4 improvements.

INSTALL
Copy all files from this ZIP into your existing Top Gun HD pack folder,
replacing files when prompted. Enable HD packs and reopen your own ROM.
No ROM or emulator is included.

SCOPE
Covers the original Mission 4 movie plus five new recordings, including
complete refueling and the transition to the later dark terrain.
The final target, ending and other unrecorded outcomes still need footage
and testing. This is an unfinished development build.

TOP GUN HD v0.12.0 — DEVELOPMENT REVIEW

NEW IN THIS BUILD
- Mission 4 briefing map and weapon-selection screen.
- Indigo-violet night sky, lavender cloud deck and dark detailed ocean.
- Terrain follows native banking, pitch and air-to-sea transition.
- Night scenery stays HD through missile impact and retry.
- Close aircraft poses and near/far boats retain HD graphics.
- Approved cockpit, live HULL, danger, aircraft and effects carry over.
- Preserves all 186 approved v0.11.2 PNGs.

INSTALL
Copy all files from this ZIP into your existing Top Gun HD pack folder,
replacing files when prompted. Enable HD packs and reopen your own ROM.
No ROM or emulator is included.

SCOPE
Covers the supplied Mission 4 recording through cloud-top and ocean combat,
damage, retries and game over. Later terrain, refueling, the space-shuttle
target and ending still need footage and testing. This is an unfinished
development build, not a full-game release.
